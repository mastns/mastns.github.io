02Selected work

Six lines of work, 2019–2026, in research labs and industry projects: from simulation studies to systems tested on real hardware.

  1. 01 · Simulate

    Controllers are trained and tested in Gazebo and MuJoCo: deep-RL trotting on slopes up to 15°; DDPG, PPO and SAC compared; a Cost-of-Transport reward. Even camera placement for gait capture is chosen in simulation.

  2. 02 · Build

    Code meets hardware: an EtherCAT master and ROS/ROS 2 middleware for the Laelaps II quadruped, a low-cost gantry-harvester prototype with 360° RGB-D scanning and visual servoing, a vineyard robot with three stereo cameras.

  3. 03 · Test

    On test beds and in the field: a synthetic vineyard, 3D-printed mushrooms on real soil, die-cast parts in a two-cobot cell, e-waste on a moving conveyor; deployments in agriculture, energy, healthcare logistics and industry.

  4. 04 · Iterate

    The same problems, revisited: slope climbing (2015), compliant terrain (2018), deep-RL slope handling (2020, CLAWAR prize), energy-efficient trotting (2022–2025); gait analysis from OpenPose to multi-camera meshes to a minimal model (2019–2025).

2023–2026Navigation & inspection

Autonomous navigation, inspection and UAV swarms

Real-time replanning without GPS from a local map that grows with vehicle speed; collision-free coverage of a disaster area by a UAV swarm; autonomous inspection of extra-high-voltage substations; and visual-inertial odometry that keeps tracking inside pipes by spraying its own fluorescent landmarks.

06About

Athanasios (Thanasis) Mastrogeorgiou works at the boundary of robotics research and industry, in Athens, Greece. He is a Senior Robotics Researcher at HERON and a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics; from 2018 to 2025 he worked in parallel in industry, as Robotics Software Engineer at IKNOWHOW and CiTARD, Senior Robotics Engineer at RobotiCo and Delivery Manager at Tech Hive Labs, where he managed nine robotics and AI projects end to end.

He takes perception, motion planning and control from high-fidelity simulation to field deployments in agriculture, healthcare logistics, energy infrastructure and industrial automation; on the way, the work passes through test beds such as a synthetic vineyard, 3D-printed mushrooms on real soil and Gazebo slopes of up to 15°. Since 2014 he has worked with the Legged Robots Team on the Laelaps II quadruped, from its EtherCAT master to deep-RL trotting, and on vineyard inspection with multi-camera SLAM; within the EU-funded SoftGrip project he helped build a low-cost mushroom-harvesting gantry.
